FAQ
Is this just a GHL account? Can’t I plug this into my existing CRM?

No.

Though LeadPhoenix may share some architecture with GHL, the snapshot is the smallest piece of the stack and does not work in isolation. The real value is in how everything is wired together: proprietary automation, third-party integrations, bespoke AI modeling, and synergistic infrastructure built cohesively to serve one singular vision.

Everything is wired not only for maximal efficiency, but for maximal compliance — built specifically so you can intelligently work thousands of leads at the same time without worrying about your number getting flagged as spam, carriers shutting it down, or TCPA violations.

LeadPhoenix won’t fit in your existing GHL junk drawer. You get a dedicated OS environment provisioned and controlled for you.

What if I’m not technical?

You don’t have to be.

The OS is built to be low-friction and intuitive. Most of it runs in the background. When you do need to touch it, we keep it simple: a few buttons, not a cockpit.

White-glove onboarding means we set up your LeadPhoenix OS for you and with you — it’s not just one call, it’s a process. By the time you start running leads, the build is already done and tested.

Does this come with leads?

No. LeadPhoenix is where you work your leads, not the lead source itself.

We are compatible with virtually any TCPA-compliant lead vendor. If you’re looking for great leads, we recommend Razor Ridge Leads — LeadPhoenix OS was designed specifically to support leads like these.

How long does setup take?

Average setup is 10–14 days from when your setup fee clears.

Your monthly subscription does not start until 30 days after setup is complete. That buffer is there so you can get used to the system, get it producing, and make money before any monthly charge hits. No double-charging. No rushed handoff.

Why do I have to apply?

Because this is white-glove service for the top 5–10 percent of insurance professionals.

We only take on as many accounts as we can onboard properly and support well. Practical floor: if you are not yet writing around $20K in annual premium per month, a cheaper CRM will serve you better. There are inexpensive ways to get good. Once you are good, come talk to us.

I’m new or struggling to make sales — will LeadPhoenix help me?

No.

LeadPhoenix is not going to make someone good at sales. The OS is built for seasoned producers who want to more efficiently monetize their book of business and generate more profit from their existing resources and workflows.

If you can’t close a door, we can’t open one for you. Get your reps in, get your skills up, then come back when you are closing and want an operating system to scale it.
